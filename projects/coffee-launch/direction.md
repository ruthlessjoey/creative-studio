# Visual Direction: Kopi Senja, Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren

status: GENERATED (placeholder brand)

## Concept
"Golden hour in a glass": the drink glows like a late-afternoon sun (senja) against deep espresso darkness.

## Mood
Warm, premium, friendly, cooling. Amber glow, condensation, ice.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | #2B1B12 | backgrounds, text on light |
| Cream | #F5E9D7 | headlines, glass, buttons |
| Amber | #D98E32 | accents, price badge, highlights |
| Palm sugar | #5A3216 | swirls, depth |

Contrast (calculated): cream on espresso 13.8:1, amber on espresso 6.2:1, espresso on amber 6.2:1. All pass 4.5:1.

## Typography
- Display: serif (Georgia fallback in SVG). Web version may use Cormorant Garamond from Google Fonts.
- Body: clean sans (Arial fallback). Web version may use Montserrat from Google Fonts.

## Do
- Keep one glowing focal point.
- Use generous dark space.
- Keep the price in a badge so it is readable in 1 second.

## Don't
- No neon colors or purple gradients.
- No more than 2 typefaces.
- No small text under 24px on a 1080-wide design.

## Voice (Bahasa Indonesia)
Short, warm, sensory: "Gula aren asli. Susu segar. Es batu penuh."
