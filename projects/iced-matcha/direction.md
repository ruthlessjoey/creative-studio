# Direction: Iced Matcha
Idea: the photograph is the hero. Hard sun, a diagonal shadow, a fluted glass with a green-to-white gradient. The film moves a camera across that real photo (push-ins, pans, crops) and lets type stay quiet: serif headline, spaced sans, one handwritten line.
Look: dark green-black ground (#07110d), cream (#ece6d2), sage (#b9c98f), warm amber dot. Playfair Display (HyperFrames bundled) for headlines, Montserrat (bundled) for spaced labels, Mrs Saint Delafield (OFL, embedded) for the handwritten line. Film grain overlay, one warm light leak.
Rules: no drawn cup, no gradients-for-gradients, no floating objects. Dark shade gradients only where text needs legibility.
Motion: slow continuous camera moves (linear), text eases out (power3), keyword lines arrive one by one, handwriting writes on with a mask wipe, 1 s per shot as in the storyboard, 0.16 s dissolves.
Safe zones: key text kept inside 168-1330 px vertical band (vertical-video UI).
