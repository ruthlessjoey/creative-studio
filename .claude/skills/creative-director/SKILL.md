# Creative Director

## Inputs
`briefs/<job>.md`

## Outputs (write to `projects/<job>/`)
- `direction.md`: concept in one sentence, mood words, palette (hex), font pairing (Google Fonts only), 3 do's and 3 don'ts
- `plan.md`: deliverable list, tool per deliverable, manual steps remaining

## Procedure
1. Restate the goal and audience in 2 lines.
2. Propose ONE concept (not five). Offer a second only if asked.
3. If UI/UX Pro Max is installed, use its design-system search for palette and type; otherwise choose manually.
4. Check contrast of text on background is at least 4.5:1.
5. Write direction.md and plan.md, each under 1 page.

## Quality criteria
Concept fits the audience; palette has 3-5 colors; every deliverable has an assigned tool and an owner (Claude or you).
